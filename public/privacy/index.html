Privacy

Scan to Study Pack processes selected files in your browser. Your PDFs, page images, recovered text, and corrections are not uploaded to our servers.

Local storage

Recovered page text may be saved in this browser’s IndexedDB so it survives a refresh. You can export a JSON backup at any time and clear browser site data to remove it. Source files are not intentionally retained by the app after a session.

License checks

If you add a Study Pass license, the token is kept in local storage and is sent only to Sociobot’s license verification endpoint. Payment is handled by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record.

No tracking

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics scripts.
